Common Questions

We track hail from 0.75" (dime size) and above. Hail at 1.00" (quarter size) can crack shingle granules and shorten your roof's lifespan. Golf ball hail (1.75") almost always causes insurance-claimable damage.
3-tab shingles are rated to about 60–70 mph, but many insurers apply a 45 mph threshold for older or weathered shingles. This tracker flags all wind events at 45 mph or higher and checks whether your address falls inside the affected area.
Most Georgia homeowners insurance policies allow storm damage claims within 2 years of the loss event. This tracker uses a rolling window that updates every day. Events older than 2 years are shown grayed out as informational history only.
